# Q3 2025 Category Profitability

**Outerwear generated the highest net revenue in fiscal Q3 2025 (July 1 – September 30) at $60,133.60 — more than any other product category once returns are factored in.**

## How we got there

We measured **net revenue**, not raw sales: the total value of every **completed** order placed during Q3 2025, minus all **refunds** issued against those orders — regardless of when the refund itself was processed. Orders marked **cancelled** or **pending** were excluded entirely, since they never generated real revenue.

## The catch the board should know

Footwear actually sold more gross merchandise than Outerwear this quarter. But Footwear also drew far more in **refunds** — about $19,600, versus roughly $3,600 for Outerwear. Once those refunds are subtracted, Footwear drops to a distant second place. Judging categories on sales volume alone would have pointed the board to the wrong answer.
